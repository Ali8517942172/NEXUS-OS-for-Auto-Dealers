Where the money goes

A dealership rarely loses a sale in one big moment. It loses it in four small ones.

None of these show up on a P&L as a line item. That is exactly why they run for years.

◷

The enquiry nobody answered

A buyer messages at 9pm. Your salesperson sees it at 9am. By then they have messaged four other showrooms, and one of them replied in a minute.

◱

The hot buyer treated like a browser

Someone asking about finance on a specific VIN and someone asking "is it available" get the same answer, from whoever is free. The serious one waits.

◔

The car quietly eating its own margin

Every day a unit sits, it costs you. By the time it feels slow, the profit you were protecting by holding the price is already gone.

◈

The conversation nobody can find

Half the business happens on personal WhatsApp. A salesperson leaves and the history of every customer they touched leaves with them.

How it works

It reads, it decides, it acts — and it writes down what it did.

NEXUS automatically responds to inbound dealership enquiries and routes high-intent opportunities to the right team.

01 — Reads

Your stock, your conversations

It connects to the inventory and enquiry data you already keep. It replaces no system you are running today — it sits above them.

02 — Decides

Grounded in your real prices

When it answers a customer, the price comes from your stock list, not from the model's imagination. It holds your cost price and is blocked from revealing it.

03 — Acts

And stops when you step in

It answers, routes, and flags ageing stock nightly. The moment one of your people joins the chat, it goes quiet.

04 — Records

Every run is checked

Each run verifies that the steps it claims to have taken actually landed, and writes an audit record either way. You can read back what happened and when.

05 — Grades itself

It tells you when it's broken

The system scores its own automations and reports the ones that are degraded or producing nothing. You are not left guessing whether it ran.

06 — Refuses to guess

Unknown is never "none"

If it can't tell two customers apart, it leaves the message unassigned rather than attaching it to the wrong person. An empty screen says why it's empty.

Why this is worth a month of your time

The gap between a fast reply and a next-morning one is the whole business.

One published figure below, named and linked so you can check it yourself, and two plain observations that are not statistics and are not dressed up as any. Nothing here is measured from your showroom — we have not seen it.

Published research

An hour late is a different business

Firms that tried to contact an online enquiry within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify it as those that tried an hour later, and more than sixty times as likely as those who waited a day. Source: Oldroyd, McElheran & Elkington, “The Short Life of Online Sales Leads”, Harvard Business Review, March 2011. It is not an automotive study and it is not from the UAE — read it as the shape of the problem, not as your number.

Observation, not a statistic

Buyers message several showrooms at once

A buyer on Dubizzle or Instagram sends the same question to four listings in a row. The showroom that answers first is the one setting the price expectation and booking the test drive. We have no survey to put behind that — it is the behaviour every dealer already describes.

Observation, not a statistic

Every day a car sits, it costs

Depreciation, floorplan and showroom space run whether the unit moves or not — arithmetic, not a finding. NEXUS recomputes days-in-stock nightly and flags units before the margin is gone. What it costs you is a number only your own figures can produce.

What we are not doing here: putting a dirham figure on your losses. We have not seen your stock, your traffic or your margins, so any number we printed would be invented. Run the free month and the dashboard will show you your own figures, measured — not estimated.

Questions

The things dealers actually ask.

Do I have to throw away my DMS or CRM?

No. NEXUS sits above the systems you already run. It reads from them, reasons across them, and acts. It replaces nothing — that is the whole point of it.

How fast does it actually reply?

Seconds to a couple of minutes. Measured in our pilot dealership across live replies: fastest 13.3 seconds, median 38.8 seconds, slowest 3 minutes 38 seconds. We publish the slow end deliberately — a vendor who only quotes their best number is telling you about their best number.

Will the AI invent a price and embarrass me?

The price it quotes is read from your stock list, not generated. It also holds your cost price and is blocked from disclosing it. In a pilot test it quoted AED 152,000 for a Fortuner — the exact asking price on that VIN — and never mentioned the cost.

What happens when my salesperson is already replying?

It checks before it writes and stays out. It is there for the hours and the messages your team cannot cover, not to talk over them.

Is my data separated from other dealerships?

Yes, at the database level. Every table holding your data carries a dealership id and every access rule checks it. It was tested with two test dealerships sharing a deliberately identical customer email and phone number — each saw only its own records. We still run one dealership per system.

How many dealerships are using NEXUS today?

One, as a pilot, in Dubai. We have no paying customers yet and no case studies to show you — which is why the audit is free and the first month is free. If you want a vendor with a wall of logos, we are not it yet. If you would rather be early and judge it on your own showroom for a month, this is the moment.

What do I need to give you to start?

Your stock list and access to the number your enquiries arrive on. Setup is done by hand, by us, and it is included — there is no setup fee.

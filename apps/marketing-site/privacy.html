Privacy policy

NEXUS for AutoDealers · last updated 21 September 2026

In one sentence. NEXUS handles a dealership's own customer conversations on that dealership's behalf. Each dealership's data is kept separate from every other dealership's. That data is never sold, never shared with advertisers or data brokers, and never used to train machine-learning models. This marketing website is a separate thing and is measured with advertising cookies — what that means is set out below.

Who we are, and who controls the data

NEXUS for AutoDealers is operated by Ali Asgher, trading as NEXUS, reachable at aliasgher892@gmail.com. NEXUS is sold to automotive dealerships as a subscription service.

The distinction that matters most: the dealership is the controller of its customers' data, and NEXUS is the processor. When a car buyer messages a dealership, that conversation belongs to the dealership. NEXUS stores and acts on it under the dealership's instruction and for no other purpose. If a dealership leaves, its data goes with it.

In plain words: the United Arab Emirates has a federal data protection law, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL). Under it, a dealership that collects and decides what to do with its customers' personal data is the controller, and a service provider that handles that data on the dealership's instruction, like NEXUS, is the processor. The obligations that fall on a controller — telling customers how their data is used, honouring their rights, having a lawful basis to process it — are the dealership's to meet. NEXUS's role is to handle the data securely, only as instructed, and to help the dealership meet those obligations. This page is not legal advice; a dealership with specific compliance questions should ask its own lawyer.

WhatsApp

Dealerships connect their own WhatsApp Business number through the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) operated by Meta. When a customer messages that number:

  • The message is delivered to NEXUS by Meta over a signed webhook. NEXUS verifies the signature and refuses anything it cannot verify.
  • NEXUS stores the message content, the sender's WhatsApp phone number, the message identifier and the timestamp, in the dealership's own records.
  • NEXUS may send a reply on the dealership's behalf — for example the asking price of a vehicle the customer asked about, read from that dealership's own stock list.
  • The moment a member of the dealership's staff joins the conversation, NEXUS stops replying.

NEXUS does not read, index or scan any conversation other than those sent to a dealership's own connected business number. It has no access to a customer's other WhatsApp chats, contacts, groups or profile beyond the phone number and display name WhatsApp itself provides with the message.

Messages are handled under Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform terms. Whether a business may message a customer, and within what window, is governed by those rules, not by NEXUS. The connecting dealership is responsible for complying with WhatsApp's and Meta's platform policies — including messaging only people who have contacted them or otherwise consented, and using approved message templates outside the 24-hour customer service window. NEXUS may suspend a dealership's messaging if its use puts NEXUS's own platform access at risk.

Email

Where a dealership connects a Google account, NEXUS is granted permission to send email as that account — to reply to customers who contacted the dealership, and to alert the dealership's own staff when a lead needs attention. Every message is one the dealership would otherwise have written by hand.

Mailbox contents are never read, indexed, downloaded or scanned. The permission granted is to send.

Keeping dealerships separate

Every record carries the identity of the dealership it belongs to, and access rules are enforced in the database itself rather than only in the application. A signed-in user of one dealership cannot read another dealership's customers, messages, stock or actions.

Artificial intelligence

NEXUS uses AI models to draft replies and to judge which enquiries need a person's attention. Two limits apply, and they are the ones that matter:

  • Prices, stock and finance figures are read from the dealership's own records. A model is never asked to supply a number that a customer could act on.
  • Customer data is not used to train models, ours or any third party's.

What is never done

  • Dealership and customer data is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers or data brokers. No name, phone number, email address, message or identity document held for a dealership is ever sent to Meta, Google or any other advertising platform — not raw, and not hashed.
  • Data is never used to train machine-learning models.
  • Data is never transferred to a third party except a service provider needed to run NEXUS (hosting, database, AI inference, the messaging platform itself), or where production is legally required.

This marketing website, and advertising

Everything above is about data a dealership entrusts to NEXUS. This section is about this website only — the pages you are reading now.

NEXUS buys advertising on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google to reach dealerships. To know which advert produced an enquiry, and therefore whether the money was worth spending, this site loads the Meta Pixel and the Google tag, which set cookies in your browser. If those tools have not been switched on for this site, no advertising script is loaded and no advertising cookie is set at all.

What is sent to Meta and Google: that a page was viewed, that a form was submitted, and that a WhatsApp link was clicked, together with a random identifier that exists only to stop the same event being counted twice.

What is never sent to Meta or Google: your name, your phone number, your email address, your dealership's name, or anything you typed into the form. Not in plain text, and not hashed. The features that would do this automatically — Meta's Automatic Advanced Matching and Google's Enhanced Conversions — are deliberately switched off.

The enquiry form is submitted as a POST request, so nothing you type ever appears in a web address, in a browser history, or in a referrer header passed to a third party.

The site also records which advert or link brought you here — the campaign tags and click identifiers on the address you arrived at, the referring site, and the page you landed on. That is stored alongside your enquiry so we know which advert to keep paying for. It is a fixed list of fields; nothing else from the address is kept.

To refuse all of this: block third-party cookies or use a content blocker, or use your browser's private mode. The enquiry form works normally either way, and refusing changes nothing about how quickly anyone replies to you.

Where data lives

On managed infrastructure: a private database (Supabase, PostgreSQL), a private virtual machine for automation, and static hosting for the dashboard. AI inference is performed by third-party model providers who receive only the text needed to produce a reply.

How long it is kept

Conversation history is kept while the dealership's relationship with that customer is active, and for as long as the dealership remains a subscriber. Execution logs — the technical record of each automated action NEXUS took, separate from the conversation itself — are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically. Identity documents are deleted automatically on a retention schedule. On termination, a dealership may request export or deletion of its data, and we delete or return it within a reasonable period of that request.

Your rights as a customer of a dealership

If you messaged a dealership and want to know what is held about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, the dealership is the right first contact — it controls that data. You may also write to aliasgher892@gmail.com and we will act on the dealership's instruction. To stop receiving WhatsApp messages, reply STOP to the dealership's number, or block it.

Google Limited Use

NEXUS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. A connected Google account can withdraw access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; access ends immediately and the stored token stops working.

### 2.7 The gate learned that time is the wrong witness for freshness

`b22a2d9`, then `fd37925`.

`L1` was red because the snapshot was stale again — `whatsapp_templates` gained
two columns **eighteen minutes** after the snapshot was taken. **Last night's

The right witness is whether the snapshot corresponds to the **migration history**
it claims to describe. The catalogue now carries a version anchor; `L1` became

differences and anchors disagree → **NOT RUN**), and it **can only move a PASS to
NOT RUN, never the other way**. `L13` (P0) is new and checks the anchor against

made by accident while making it.

`L2` arm 2 also went from a token to a real test. Postgres stores `USING (1=1)`
as `(1 = 1)` and does not fold it, so an open policy could be written in a way the
check could not see. Openness is now decided by Postgres — no `{VAR` node in the
parse tree, then evaluate — fenced against function calls and sublinks, with
anything the fence excludes reported as a new WARN `L2c` rather than treated as
closed. **Both projects: 12 open before, 12 after, 0 gained, 0 lost, 0
undecidable** — which changes nothing today, and that is the correct outcome for

## 3 · Five places a previous claim turned out to be wrong

### 3.1 A **false CLEAR** on the flagship screen, on the day it shipped

`screens/money-leaks.js` tested `sla_state === 'BREACHED'`. `v_lead_recovery`
emits `WITHIN_SLA` and `BREACHED_SLA` **and nothing else**, so the filter was
**structurally incapable of matching** and the screen printed *"No open enquiry
is recorded as having missed the first-response target"* **unconditionally** —
while naming the same breached enquiries by name in register 4 on the same page.
On the demo dataset, six of eleven open enquiries were breached, at 7, 12, 41, 18,
95 and 6 minutes against a five-minute target.

**It was invisible on production**, which has one open lead and has never emitted
`BREACHED_SLA`. A control this project cares about more than any other was wrong
for an hour, and only a second dealership's data could show it.

Fixed as a rule and not as a string: the vocabulary is named, a value outside it
renders a named FAULT rather than passing silently, and the verdict is decided by
**complement** — *"within the target"* is a positive assertion the view has to
make, and everything else is a breach or an unknown, neither of which may render
as clear. The denominator became what was actually **measurable** rather than
what was open.

`ops/DEMO.md` opens by telling the presenter the clear register contained a wrong
line, rather than shaping the data to hide it.

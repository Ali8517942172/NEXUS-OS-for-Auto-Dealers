## The rule every line below obeys

Every sentence is one of exactly three things:

1. **A capability claim** — what the software does. Present tense, mechanical,
   checkable against `PRODUCT.md` and the landing page's own "What it does"
   section: *answers, scores, routes, recomputes, flags, records, goes silent
   when a human steps in.*
2. **A question** — about the reader's situation, never an assertion about it.
3. **A disclosed fact about the offer** — AED 399/month, first month free, no
   setup fee, no minimum term, no contract, free audit, built in Dubai, one pilot
   dealership, no paying customers yet.

**The offer of record (decided 17 September 2026): AED 399 per month, flat and
permanent, first month free, no setup fee, no minimum term.** There is no cap on
how many dealerships may take it and no expiry date.

**No scarcity, ever.** No "only 10 places", no "first 10 dealerships", no "until
31 October", no countdown, no "founding rate", no "price goes up soon". Earlier
drafts of the offer carried a cap and a deadline; **neither exists now**, so any
such line would be manufactured urgency — prohibited by Meta Advertising
Standards (`POLICY-RISK.md` §3) and untrue besides. The only urgency this funnel
is allowed is the dealer's own unanswered enquiries.

**Nothing below is a result claim.** No recovered revenue. No percentage
uplift. No "dealers using NEXUS see…". No testimonial. No named dealership.
No logo. **ALBA Cars does not appear anywhere** — it is tenant #1 and the
pilot, not a customer and not a reference.

### Phrases that must never be written

> "NEXUS recovered AED X" · "10x your leads" · "Our dealers close 40% more" ·
> "Trusted by UAE's leading showrooms" · "Guaranteed ROI" · "Join 100+ dealers" ·
> "Ahmed from Al Quoz says…" · "Don't lose another sale" (a claim about the
> reader's losses) · anything with a countdown, "only 3 spots", or a fake
> deadline · any manufacturer or marketplace trademark (Toyota, Dubizzle,
> YallaMotor) · the ALBA Cars name or premises.

### Voice note

Third person and category framing, not second-person assertions about the
reader — "Showrooms in Dubai…" rather than "Your showroom is losing…". This
is both better copy for a skeptical owner and the safe side of Meta's
Personal Attributes policy (see POLICY-RISK.md §2).

**Note for the file:** the five-minute finding is an industry statistic, not
ours. It is cited on the landing page in the same terms

uncited percentage is an unsubstantiated claim under Meta's rules.

Privacy policy

NEXUS OS · last updated 21 September 2026

In one sentence: NEXUS OS is a subscription service for automotive dealerships in the UAE. Each dealership's data is kept separate from every other dealership's, it is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used to train machine-learning models.

Who operates this, and who controls the data

NEXUS OS is operated by Ali Asgher, trading as NEXUS, reachable at aliasgher892@gmail.com. It is sold as a subscription to automotive dealerships in the UAE. The subscribing dealership is the controller of its customers' data; NEXUS is the processor. Under the UAE's federal data protection law (PDPL, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), the controller is the one who decides how and why customer data is used, and the processor handles it only on the controller's instruction. NEXUS stores and acts on a dealership's data under that dealership's instruction and for no other purpose. This page is not legal advice.

Channels: WhatsApp and email

A dealership connects its own WhatsApp Business number through the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) operated by Meta, and/or its own Google account. When a customer messages the dealership's WhatsApp number, the message is delivered to NEXUS by Meta over a signed webhook, which NEXUS verifies before acting on it. Where a dealership connects a Google account, NEXUS is granted permission to send email as that account — to reply to customers and to alert staff when a lead needs attention. Mailbox contents are never read, indexed or scanned; the permission granted is to send. The connecting dealership is responsible for complying with WhatsApp's and Meta's platform policies, including messaging only people who have contacted them or otherwise consented.

Keeping dealerships separate

Every record carries the identity of the dealership it belongs to, and access is scoped so that one dealership's staff cannot read another dealership's customers, messages, stock or actions.

What is stored

  • Customer name, phone number, email address, message content and timestamps, and vehicle/enquiry details — so the dealership can identify, contact and follow up with the person who enquired.
  • An OAuth token or WhatsApp connection credential for a connected account, held encrypted, never transmitted elsewhere.
  • A record of every automated action taken (an execution log), so a dealership can audit what was done on its behalf.

What is never done

  • Dealership and customer data is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • It is never used to train machine-learning models, ours or anyone else's.
  • It is never transferred to a third party except a service provider needed to run NEXUS (hosting, database, AI inference, the messaging platform itself), or where production is legally required.

Where data lives

On managed infrastructure: a private database (Supabase, PostgreSQL) and a private automation server. AI inference is performed by third-party model providers who receive only the text needed to produce a reply.

How long it is kept

Conversation history is kept while the dealership's relationship with that customer is active, and for as long as the dealership remains a subscriber. Execution logs are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically. Identity documents collected for the compliance checks a vehicle sale requires are deleted automatically on a retention schedule. On termination, a dealership may request export or deletion of its data.

Revoking access

A connected Google account can withdraw NEXUS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Access ends immediately and the stored token stops working.

Limited Use

NEXUS OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights as a customer of a dealership

If you messaged a dealership and want to know what is held about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, the dealership is the right first contact — it controls that data. You may also write to aliasgher892@gmail.com and we will act on the dealership's instruction.
